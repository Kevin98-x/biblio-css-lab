Shelf questions

Can I buy a book outright instead of borrowing?

Yes. Pay the price shown and it's yours to keep, with no return and no refund needed. Ask at the desk or note it on the deposit page.

What if the book I want has zero copies?

Add yourself to the waitlist on the contact page and we'll message you the moment a copy returns.

How is a book's band decided?

Our desk grades every book on intake against the same condition checklist used at borrow and return. The band is written inside the front cover.
