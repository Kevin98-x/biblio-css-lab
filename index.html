The deposit, explained

Borrowing here isn't a fee — it's a promise you can take back. The full retail price of the book is held as a deposit while it lives on your nightstand.

  1. Checkout. We record the book's exact condition on a checklist — cover, spine, pages, markings, the lot — and you pay the deposit.
  2. Keep it. Read at your own pace. There's no due date, only the promise to bring it back.
  3. Return. We re-check the book against the same checklist. If it matches, your full deposit is refunded on the spot.

FAQ: what if the condition slips? Minor wear is expected and forgiven. Significant damage is deducted against the deposit at the rate shown on your checklist.

What counts as a fair swap?

Books are grouped into bands by age and condition. Any book may be swapped for another in the same band. The desk will tell you a book's band when you bring it in.
